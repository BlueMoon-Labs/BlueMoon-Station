code/modules/mob/living/ventcrawling.dm

/proc/collect_pipes_in_viewCollects members of pipenet_members whose turf lies inside the square box of half-size view_half around source_turf, appending them to output. The bounds are hoisted out of the loop on purpose: the old path called in_view_range() -> getviewsize() (a proc call plus a list allocation) once per pipe, which on a station distro loop is thousands of calls per ventcrawl step (perf.log: 750k in_view_range calls per round).
