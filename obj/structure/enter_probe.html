enter_probe
/obj/structure/enter_probe

Tests for who gets consulted when a movable enters a turf.

/turf/Enter walks the destination's contents and calls Cross() on everything standing there. The walk order is contents order, which is creation order, not priority: the real priority is assembled in firstbump by layer and by ON_BORDER_1. None of that had a test, and the walk cannot be rewritten without one.

The exit side of the same contract lives in turf_exit_checks.dm. A dense probe that always refuses, counting both how often it was asked and how often something ran into it.
