The magazine and the chambered casing live in our contents, so their loc is a hard reference back to us. Without clearing them a qdeleted gun never soft-GCs and every single one ends up hard-deleted. Cheap normally, brutal for guns that delete themselves per shot (DROPDEL enchanted rifles - the Arcane Barrage volley was the top hard-delete source in round logs).
