resumable_cost_counter
/datum/resumable_cost_counter

A cost counter for resumable, repeating processes (Paradise port). The MC's per-fire cost average hides the true length of a multi-tick pass: a phase that sleeps across 8 ticks reports 8 small slices instead of one real total. record_progress() accumulates slices until the pass finishes.

Procs

record_progressUpdates the counter based on the time spent making progress and whether the task finished.
to_stringDisplay string: last completed pass total, or "+" while an even longer pass is in progress.
