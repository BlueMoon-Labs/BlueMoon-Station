Candy
/mob/living/simple_animal/hostile/asteroid/elite/candy

Kind of like bubblegum's rebellious teenage son/daughter. Has 4 attacks. Charge - Charges at it's target. Bloody Trap - Traps it's target between some walls, and then charges at them. Meat Shield - Knockbacks all targets in the 3 tiles he faces, and then creates a wall. Knockdown - Deals damage and knockbacks all targets in a 2 tile radius.
